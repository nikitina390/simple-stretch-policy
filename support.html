Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. Simple Daily Stretch works without any sign-up or login—just open the app and start a session.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

You can manage notification permissions any time in your device's Settings → Notifications → Simple Stretch.

I'm not receiving notifications.

Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in iOS Settings, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. Reopening the app once also refreshes your reminder settings.

Are there any ads or subscriptions?

No ads, and no subscription required to follow along with the guided routines.

How is my data handled?

Your profile, streak, and history are stored on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes the on-device data. To request deletion of any server-side records (such as a push token or feedback you submitted), email us at the address above.
